Brand & content rules

ELI core message
Improve your English. Build your future.

ELI content rules

  1. Large headlines and minimal text on artwork.
  2. Consistent North Central College identity on every post.
  3. Alternate the institutional palettes across posts: red, black, white emphasis.
  4. Instagram captions must be in English, contain no dashes, and include exactly three hashtags and two to three emojis.
  5. Track both the 3:4 artwork and the 9:16 Story summary.
  6. Scheduled and Published are planning statuses. This planner does not post to social media.

  • Pantone 200 leads. Its 20% and 40% shades, a 50% tint of 635 C, and 50% / 10% grays are allowed as supporting colors.
  • Only #c10230 and #212121 pass WCAG AA for text. Do not set body copy in the light blue or Red 32.

Typography (Section 3.2)

  • GT Pressura (headlines, subheads, body), GT Pressura Mono (headlines and subheads only) and Newport (handwritten accent) are licensed to the Office of Marketing only.
  • Free alternatives for everyone: Roboto, Roboto Mono and Permanent Marker. System fallback: Arial, Courier New, Georgia.
  • Use the accent font sparingly: short words or call outs only, never body copy, never below 14 pt, slightly wider tracking.
  • Underline a key word in a headline for emphasis. Keep leading tight but not cramped.

Logo (Section 1)

  • Use the official files only. Never redraw, stretch, tilt, recolor, add shadows, rearrange, or mimic the wordmark with brand fonts.
  • Clear space all around: the width of “1861”. Minimum on screen: tower 25 px tall; use the wordmark alone when space is tight (stacked wordmark under 960 px wide).
  • Never place the white logo on a blue background or over a busy photo.
  • International recruiting (most ELI audiences): use the International logo (“North Central 1861 · Naperville’s University”, without the word College). Request it from the Office of Institutional Communication, oic@noctrl.edu.
  • Files in this planner: horizontal + tagline (SVG) · white version (SVG) · horizontal (PNG) · stacked (PNG)

Design elements & photos (Sections 3.4 to 3.7)

  • Approved accents: underlined key words, short handwritten notes and marks, pattern blocks, the “track” pattern, the Cardinal. Use them to frame, not to crowd.
  • Photos: real students, natural light. Duotones (635 C background + 7689 C foreground, or Pantone 200 on white) and single color overlays on black and white photos are on brand.
  • Tagline: “Naperville’s University”. Outreach units use the logo with tagline.

Caption checks in this planner only flag issues. They never rewrite anyone's copy.

Source: North Central College Graphic Identity & Messaging Guidelines (2026 edition, ELI Web/media/ncc-brand-guidelines.pdf) and the official logo files supplied by the ELI team.
